About Mysticia

Mysticia is an independent Spanish-language channel of short videos about unsolved mysteries, historical enigmas and conspiracies that turned out to be real and are publicly documented (for example declassified programs such as MKUltra or Operation Northwoods). Theories are always presented as theories, and documented facts are clearly separated from speculation.

Watch the channel on YouTube: youtube.com/@mysticiatv

Mysticia Autopilot

Mysticia Autopilot is the private production tool used by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos. It runs only on the owner's computer, is not offered to other users, and acts exclusively on the Mysticia channel after the owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0).

This tool uses YouTube API Services. It uses the YouTube Data API to:
  • upload the channel's own original videos (videos.insert), declared as AI-assisted / synthetic content and not made for kids;
  • read public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos (videos.list) to decide which topics to produce next;
  • read the public "most popular" chart (videos.list, chart=mostPopular) as a signal of audience interests. Titles are used only in memory and are not stored.
By using YouTube features through this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy (which links to the Google Privacy Policy) and our Terms of Service.
