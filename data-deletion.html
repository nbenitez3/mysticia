Last updated: October 10, 2026

Mysticia Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Mysticia channel and Page. It stores, on the owner's computer only, the access tokens needed to publish and statistics of the channel's own videos (statistics are deleted automatically after 30 days). It stores no data about other people.

Remove access from Facebook

  1. Open Facebook Settings → Business integrations.
  2. Select Mysticia Autopilot and choose Remove.

Remove access from Google / YouTube

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/connections.
  2. Select Mysticia Autopilot and choose Delete all connections.

Request deletion of stored data

Send an email to nbenitezr32@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". All locally stored tokens and statistics related to you are deleted within 7 days and you receive a confirmation by email.
